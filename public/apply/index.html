What happens next

  • 1We read itIt goes to the people who review it, and is treated with discretion.
  • 2We reach outSomeone from the fund contacts you to understand what would genuinely help.
  • 3We work out termsIf we can help, we agree on repayment together. We will say so straight away if we cannot.

What we never ask for

  • Your Social Security number
  • Bank logins or account numbers
  • Documents uploaded through this form

We will never ask for these — not by email, text, or phone.

Not sure if you qualify?

There is no qualifying, and no threshold to clear. If money is the problem, ask — the worst outcome is that we say we cannot help this time.
