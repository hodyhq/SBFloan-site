Why we exist

Nobody should have to explain their hardest month to someone who has a stake in the answer.

Most people in a tight spot already know where they could ask. What stops them is everything attached to asking — who finds out, who feels owed, and what it means next time they walk into shul.

SBF was built to remove all of that. We are a nonprofit, independent of any shul or political group, funded by donations from people in this community.

How it works

Three steps, and none
of them are difficult.

1

You apply

One form, from your phone. It goes to the people who review it — not to a shul, and not onto any list.

2

We talk it through

Someone from the fund reaches out to understand the situation. A conversation, and you set the pace.

3

Funds go out

If we can help, we agree on repayment together before anything moves.

Common questions

Before you apply.

Do I need to be a member of a shul to apply?

No. The Sepharadic Baltimore Fund is independent of any shul or political group. You do not need to be a member anywhere, know anyone, or be owed a favor.

Is my application confidential?

Your application goes to the people who review it and is treated with discretion. It does not go to a shul and it does not go onto any list.

What if I cannot repay?

Repayment is discussed with you before anything is agreed. We cannot help with everything, and we will say so straight away if we cannot.

What can I ask for help with?

Rent and mortgage, a bar or bat mitzvah, a wedding, finding a job, credit card debt, or something else entirely. There is no threshold to clear.
